How to Compress Image to 20KB Online Without Losing Quality

Government recruitment portals (SSC, UPSC, Banking, Railways) frequently reject files with the error "Image file size must be between 20 KB and 50 KB". Compressing with ordinary apps causes blurred eyes or pixelated signatures:

  1. Targeted Bicubic Resampling: Instead of shrinking files randomly, the browser resamples to the exact physical pixel boundary (e.g. 350x450 px).
  2. Iterative DCT Quantization: Tests compression tables from 0.98 down to 0.05 to land within 1KB to 2KB below the ceiling.
  3. Zero EXIF Overhead: Strips GPS and camera lens tags, dedicating 100% of the allowable 50KB budget to pure facial sharpness.

IBPS & Railway Recruitment Biometric Rules
